Meet the founder

{{ site.brand.founder }}

Freelancer, Fiverr Top Rated Seller, and the founder of {{ site.brand.company }}.

Bevin did not start with a design degree or a network. He started as a photo editor with a laptop and a Fiverr account, and waited almost a full year for his first order.

Instead of quitting, he studied what actually got sellers chosen: positioning, portfolio, pricing and pitching. He turned it into a repeatable system. The orders came, then Top Rated status, then students asking how he did it.

That system became LEAP, and Freelancer College is where it is taught, in Sinhala, to people who want real skills and real income.
