LEAP

Learn. Execute. Assemble. Pitch. The system that took Bevin from a year of zero orders to Fiverr Top Rated, turned into four moves anyone can follow.

{{> leap }}

Each move, in depth

What each step actually means.

L

Learn

Real skills from zero, in Sinhala, taught the way the job actually uses them.

  • Only what clients pay for, not academic theory
  • Explained in Sinhala, with the English terms you will use with clients
  • Short lessons, then practice immediately
  • Mistake to avoid: collecting tutorials instead of building
E

Execute

Build real projects, not exercises. You practice on work that looks like client work.

  • Real briefs with constraints and deadlines
  • Feedback on every assignment
  • Live client style projects you can show
  • Mistake to avoid: redesigning Netflix for the tenth time
A

Assemble

Shape that work into a portfolio and a profile that gets chosen.

  • Case studies that show thinking, not just screens
  • Fiverr and Upwork profiles built to convert
  • Gig titles, thumbnails and packages that get clicks
  • Mistake to avoid: a portfolio of 20 unrelated pieces
P

Pitch

Position, price and close. Win clients like a working freelancer, not a beginner.

  • Price on value, in USD. Never work for less
  • Proposals and replies that sound professional
  • Getting paid from Sri Lanka, step by step
  • Mistake to avoid: competing on the cheapest price
